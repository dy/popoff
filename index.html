popoff

Tooltips, dropdowns, hover cards and modals, on the platform's own popover and dialog.

Tooltips

Any title: the name, the keys, a line of what it does. The next waits as the first did.

<button title="Play (Space)&#10;Hold and drag to rewind">▶</button>

Dropdowns

data-place puts a popover by what opened it, with CSS anchor positioning.

<button popovertarget="file">File</button>
<div id="file" popover data-place="bottom start">…</div>

Hover cards

interestfor: shown while the pointer or the focus rests on it.

@dy
Dmitry Iv. sprae, popoff, audio, and other small tools. 
<a href="/dy" interestfor="dy">@dy</a>
<div id="dy" popover="hint" data-place="top">…</div>

Modals

<dialog closedby="any">: a click on the backdrop closes it.

Hello
